# Edmonton Food Drive Optimization

Edmonton's food donation system faces logistical challenges, inspiring the development of a user-friendly mobile app. This app aims to enhance the efficiency and timeliness of donation collection through data collection, resource allocation, and user-friendly features. Additionally, it offers support for data-driven decisions.
The goal is to build an app that will foster community engagement through an accessible and efficient tool for participation.

After evaluating various traditional models, RandomForest Regressor was chosen as the best performing model.

**Link to App:** https://cicanfooddriveapp-4dj59tb6mgxswynbxt3nnx.streamlit.app/

The proposed app predicts the number of donation bags and number of volunteers required for each route. Our interactive map, powered by Google My Maps, combines the City of Edmonton property assessment dataset with our own data to provide geographical locations. This merge allows us to visualize and analyze the distribution of resources across different areas of the city effectively. We have integrated the interactive Food Drive Assistant chatbot, powered by the Gradient Based model, specifically "nous-hermes2" base model, into our app. This advanced model enhances the chatbot's ability to understand and respond to user inquiries effectively, providing valuable information and improving user engagement.


In conclusion, our project used data insights to improve how we allocate key resources—volunteers, time, money, and effort—for the food drive. We navigated data complexities and followed responsible AI practices. The result: actionable insights for more efficient and impactful service delivery.
